A message of vision & purpose

Letter from leadership
SPORT
BUILDS
NATIONS
Youth Sport Development (SL) LG was born from a simple conviction: that Sierra Leone's brightest sporting talents deserve the chance to be discovered, nurtured, and celebrated.

In 2025, we took a bold step — launching our Primary School Tour across Freetown, bringing sport directly into the lives of young pupils and showing them that a career on the pitch, the court, or in the press box is not just a dream, but a real pathway.

At a time when the government is investing heavily in sport through Free Quality Education and national sport initiatives, we believe the moment to act is now. The mantra guiding our work is simple: Catch them when they are young.

Sierra Leone has already produced legends. Mohamed Kallon rose from poverty to international stardom. Abdulia Juma Bah, just 19, became the first Sierra Leonean to debut in La Liga. These stories are not exceptions — they are the beginning of a movement.

With dedication, hard work, perseverance, the right guidance, and a strong educational foundation, anything is possible in the world of sport.

THE PRIMARY
SCHOOL TOUR
Youth Sport Development SL set out on a landmark primary school tour across Freetown from October 10–28, 2025 — visiting fifteen schools with a 90–120 minute interactive programme designed to open young minds to the world of sport.
Education First
Emphasising how sports careers complement academic achievements and showing pupils both paths can thrive together.
Career Horizons
Introducing diverse opportunities: sports management, journalism, physiotherapy, nutrition, and psychology.
Role Models
Meet-and-greet sessions with professionals and athletes sharing real journeys and inspiring stories.
Hands-On Play
Practical demonstrations with footballs, basketballs, volleyballs, and tennis equipment — sport you can touch.
Stakeholder engagement
Building partnerships
